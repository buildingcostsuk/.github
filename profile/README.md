## 📐 About the Organization

Traditional UK building directories and material merchants often gate basic estimation math behind postcode paywalls, mandatory phone numbers, and lead-generation forms. 

**BuildingCosts.co.uk** was built to provide self-builders, DIY homeowners, tradespeople, and estimators with instant, transparent volumetric calculations and regional cost indices without lead walls or subscriptions.

### Calibration & Data Benchmarks
All calculations across our web engines and open libraries are calibrated against:
- **Civil Engineering Standards:** Concrete volumes and mix designs comply with **BS 8500-1 / BS 8500-2**; metric brick and mortar quantities comply with **BS EN 771-1**.
- **UK Building Regulations:** Thermal minimums modelled on **Part L**, with sound/party-wall compliance under **Part E**.
- **Material Pricing:** Calibrated quarterly against current trade list pricing from national UK builders' merchants (Travis Perkins, Wickes, B&Q, Jewson).
- **Regional Cost Variations:** Rebuild and extension estimators incorporate **RICS / BCIS** location indices across all 11 UK regions.
